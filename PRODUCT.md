# VeedUndo — Kerala Rental Board

## Register
Product (app UI) — a hyperlocal rental classifieds board for Kerala, India.

## Target Users
- Renters: Young professionals, families in Kerala looking for houses to rent
- Poster: Property owners and brokers listing available rental properties
- Primary language context: Malayalam-speaking, English UI

## Product Purpose
Solve the problem of scattered, unreliable rental information in Kerala's towns. Currently people rely on word-of-mouth, WhatsApp groups, and physical notice boards. VeedUndo centralizes rental listings with structured data (locality, rent range, house type) and community-driven quality (crowd flags, auto-expiry).

## Brand Personality
Warm, local, trustworthy, simple. Not a corporate tech product — a community tool. The name "VeedUndo" (Malayalam: "Is there a house?") signals local identity.

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics
- Overly corporate/blue color schemes
- Complex multi-step onboarding
- Feature-bloated marketplace UIs

## Strategic Design Principles
1. **Zero learning curve** — Anyone who can use WhatsApp can use VeedUndo
2. **Trust through transparency** — Owner/broker badges, expiry dates, crowd flags
3. **Local-first** — Structured locality data, not free-text search
4. **Mobile-primary** — Designed for phone usage in Kerala's mobile-first market
